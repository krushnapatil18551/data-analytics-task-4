# Task 4 – Statistical Validation

## Hypothesis
**H0:** Average order sales are equal between Female and Male.

**H1:** Average order sales are different between Female and Male.

## Test
Welch independent-samples t-test.

- Female mean: 136,883.21
- Male mean: 141,807.34
- t-statistic: -0.6826
- p-value: 0.495011
- Alpha: 0.05

## Conclusion
Fail to reject H0: the observed difference is not statistically significant.
