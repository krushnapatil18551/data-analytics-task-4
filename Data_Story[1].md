# Data Story – ApexPlanet

## Objective
Synthesize Tasks 1–3 into a business narrative.

## Story
The dataset contains 1,000 orders and generates total sales of 139,399,439.65. The average order value is 139,399.44, with 947 unique customers and 5,435 units sold.

Sales are concentrated in **Electronics** as the leading category, **Laptop** as the leading product and **Patna** as the leading city.

The Task 3 dashboard can be used to drill down by city, category and gender.

## Statistical Validation
A Welch t-test was used to compare average order sales between Female and Male. The p-value was 0.495011. At α=0.05, the result is: **not statistically significant**.

## Call to Action
Focus on high-performing categories/products/cities, monitor monthly trends, and use statistical evidence together with business impact before making decisions.
